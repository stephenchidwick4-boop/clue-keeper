How does the magic work?

Every letter has a number: A is 0, B is 1, C is 2... all the way to Z, which is 25. The secret word tells each letter of your message how far to jump along the alphabet. Encoding jumps forward, decoding jumps back. If you fall off the end, you wrap around to the start, like a clock.
